FAQ

Questions we hear often

How much does a kitchen renovation cost?

Most of our kitchens fall between $25,000 and $60,000 depending on size, cabinetry and appliances. You get a fixed-price proposal before any work starts.

How long does a typical project take?

Design takes two to three weeks. Build time ranges from four weeks for a bathroom to three months for a whole-home renovation.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. Our project managers prepare and submit all permit applications and coordinate inspections.

Can I live at home during the renovation?

Usually, yes. We plan the work in phases and keep dust-controlled zones so you can stay in your home.

Ready to start your project?

Chat with our design assistant — it can answer questions, check availability and set up your free consultation.
